# Nexus page copy (paste into mod page editor)

**Name:** Malorian Arms Dioscuri — Iconic Smart Sniper Rifle

**Short description (250 chars):**
Adds the Dioscuri, an iconic Malorian Arms smart sniper that locks two targets
and fires paired rounds. Custom kitbash model, integral silencer, level-scaled
crafting spec found in a lootable case at the old Malorian factory site.

---

## Full description (BBCode)

```
[size=5][b]MALORIAN ARMS DIOSCURI[/b][/size]

[i]Before the 3516 ever bore Johnny Silverhand's name, Eran Malor chased a
stranger obsession: a rifle that fired in pairs. Factory rumor said it was for
the twin brother who died in infancy — one trigger, two rounds, so no shot
would ever be alone. The Dioscuri never shipped. One case survived the
factory's closing.[/i]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Twin-lock smart targeting[/b] — locks onto up to TWO targets at once and
fires a 2-round burst. Both rounds are real: two chambers, two locks, one pull.
[*][b]Integral suppressor[/b] — always silenced, no ricochet, works with stealth
damage bonuses.
[*][b]True iconic[/b] — 250% headshot multiplier, tuned damage, +25% smart
reticle size, faster smart projectiles, iconic mod slot.
[*][b]Custom model[/b] — a kitbash of the Tsunami Ashura and Malorian 3516 with
engraved MALORIAN ARMS wordmark, the seven-dot logo, deep red grip, matte black
lines, and the Overwatch suppressor. Custom inventory icon and HUD holo outline.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How to get it[/b][/size]
Find the surviving Malorian gun case at the company's abandoned factory site on
the City Center northeast waterfront, below the elevated walkways. Inside is a
crafting spec whose tier matches your level (under 9 → Tier 1 ... 33+ → Tier 5),
exactly like vanilla iconic specs — single material type to craft.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]RED4ext
[*]ArchiveXL
[*]TweakXL
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Vortex: install like any other mod. Manual: extract the zip into the Cyberpunk
2077 root folder (it contains archive\pc\mod and r6\tweaks).

[size=4][b]Uninstallation[/b][/size]
Remove Dioscuri.archive, Dioscuri.archive.xl and r6\tweaks\Dioscuri. Un-equip
the weapon before uninstalling to be safe.

[size=4][b]Known notes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]The case's contents are rolled when you first open it; the spec tier is set
by your level at that moment.
[*]No scope slot by design — the game's scope overlays fight the smart-link UI.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Credits[/b][/size]
Built with WolvenKit, TweakXL, ArchiveXL, Blender + Cyberpunk IO Suite, and
MLSetupBuilder. Base meshes and materials by CD PROJEKT RED.
```
